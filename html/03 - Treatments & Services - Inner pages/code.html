Frequently Asked Questions About Routine Check-ups

Clear answers to common questions about dental examinations, NHS fees, and patient comfort.

For most adult patients with good oral stability, a check-up every 6 months is strongly recommended by NICE guidelines. However, if your gums and teeth are in pristine condition, your dentist may extend this to every 12 months. Patients with ongoing gum treatment or orthodontic appliances may be advised to attend every 3 to 4 months.
Yes, standard examinations are covered under NHS Band 1 at £26.80. This covers diagnosis, clinical radiographs, preventative advice, and a scale and polish if clinically necessary. Patients under 18 (or under 19 in full-time education), pregnant mothers, and those receiving qualifying benefits receive free NHS treatment.
Never. Routine examinations are completely non-invasive, gentle, and gentle. We use smooth, sterile instruments and gentle intraoral mirrors. We explain every action before proceeding so there are zero surprises.
Please let our reception team and clinician know! Acocks Green Dental Practice is renowned for phobic-friendly care. We provide unhurried appointments, agree on clear "stop-signals" (raising your hand immediately pauses everything), and never judge how long it has been since your last visit.
Modern digital dental radiographs emit up to 90% less radiation than older traditional film units. In fact, two standard bitewing digital X-rays expose you to less radiation than a short commercial flight or natural background radiation in the UK.
