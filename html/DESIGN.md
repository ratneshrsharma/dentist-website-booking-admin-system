## Brand & Style

The design system embodies modern clinical precision, warmth, and patient-centered trust. Rooted in community dental healthcare, it blends the established clover green heritage with a streamlined, contemporary medical aesthetic inspired by structured clinical digital portals. 

The aesthetic is characterized as **Corporate Clinical Modern with Tactile Refinement**:
- **Trust & Assurance:** High-clarity layouts with crisp, luminous white card containers, clear hierarchy, and reassuring medical green tones.
- **Structural Clarity:** Organized service grids, distinct interactive callouts, floating quick-appointment booking components, and accordion-based patient education tools.
- **Approachability:** Softened corners, generous whitespace, legible open typography, and subdued clinical tinting that removes fear and anxiety from dental care.

## Colors

The palette transforms clinical blue conventions into a distinctive, health-focused clover emerald scheme:
- **Primary (`#2E7D32`):** Grounded emerald clover green used for core actions, main headings, key button elements, and primary icons.
- **Secondary (`#0D2E1A`):** Deep botanical forest navy, providing grounding contrast for footers, hero overlays, dark booking panels, and prominent display headings.
- **Tertiary (`#43A047`):** Vivid leaf green for interactive accents, badges, highlights, and subtle gradients.
- **Neutral (`#1B2420`):** Soft charcoal with a micro-dose of green tint to avoid harsh pure blacks on body text.
- **Surfaces & Accents:**
  - `surface-clinical` (`#F6F9F7`): High-hygiene, pale mint-tinted backdrop for section transitions.
  - `brand-accent-glow` (`#E8F5E9`): Pill chip backgrounds, category pill tags, and soft icon hover fills.
  - `appointment-card-bg` (`#0D2E1A`): High-impact quick-appointment callout containers.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for clear, geometry-grounded display hierarchy with **Work Sans** for balanced reading comfort across medical copy.

- **Eyebrow Headers:** Uppercase tracked `subheading-eyebrow` in `#2E7D32` introduces sections cleanly (e.g., "OUR SERVICES", "BOOK AN APPOINTMENT").
- **Display & Headlines:** Weighted heavily (`700` and `800`) to anchor dense medical offerings and instill patient confidence.
- **Body & Information:** Regular weight (`400`) at generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) prevents clinical exhaustion when patients digest procedural details.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column responsive layout is utilized for desktop surfaces, transitioning to an 8-column layout for tablets and a 4-column stack for mobile.

- **Section Margins:** Default section padding alternates between `4rem` and `6rem` vertical breathing room to partition content blocks clearly without jarring dividers.
- **Card Spacing:** Standard service cards and pricing tiers adhere strictly to `space-lg` padding internally, ensuring medical icons, headings, and supporting details breathe comfortably.
- **Floating Overlays:** Floating appointment booking strips and contact cards utilize a negative top margin (e.g., `-3rem`) overlapping hero or banner images to provide depth and fast-action access.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean, low-saturation ambient shadow models paired with delicate structural borders:

- **Level 0 (Flat/Base):** `surface-clinical` or clean white canvas with zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Service & Content Cards):** Low-contrast border (`1px solid #E5EFE7`) with an ambient feather shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(13, 46, 26, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Cards):** Lifted card state on interaction: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.12)`, moving `translateY(-4px)` to indicate clickable agency.
- **Level 3 (Floating Appointment Quick-Forms & Popovers):** High-density clinical contrast with directional depth: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(13, 46, 26, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Level 2 (Rounded)** for standard components (`8px / 0.5rem`), transitioning up to `12px` and `16px` on larger container cards:

- **Buttons & Form Fields:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius, balancing crisp clinical precision with soft, approachable interaction.
- **Pills & Status Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for metadata, opening hours, and category labels.
- **Cards & Modals:** `12px` to `16px` radius with clipped corner containment for images, avoiding harsh brutalist corners.
- **Icon Badges:** Soft circular or rounded squircle backgrounds (`12px`) filled with tint `#E8F5E9` to house emerald line iconography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid `#2E7D32` background with `#FFFFFF` text, `8px` border radius, `font-weight: 600`, with subtle green box-shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.3)`). Hover triggers `#1B5E20` with a slight elevation lift.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #2E7D32`, text `#2E7D32`. On hover, fills with `#E8F5E9`.
- **Contrast / Emergency Callout:** Deep `#0D2E1A` background with crisp white or emerald typography for high-priority emergency bookings.

### Service Grid Cards
- **Structure:** White background, `1px solid #E5EFE7` border, `16px` corner radius.
- **Layout:** Vertical stack featuring an upper-right or centered icon medallion (`48x48px` in `#E8F5E9` with `#2E7D32` icon glyph), bold service title (`headline-sm`), short explanatory body copy, and a small terminal text link ("Read More →").
- **Hover:** Shifts border to `#43A047` and activates Level 2 elevation.

### Floating Quick-Appointment Card
- **Structure:** Dark emerald container (`#0D2E1A`) or crisp white container overlapping the hero section, featuring a 3-field compact form layout (Full Name, Phone/Email, Service Select, and Action Button).
- **Controls:** Input elements styled with clean contrast, white or subtle field backgrounds, and custom green chevron dropdowns.

### FAQ Accordions
- **Header:** White card container with bold text, containing a right-aligned square icon button (`36x36px`) in primary green with a white plus/minus or chevron icon.
- **Expanded Panel:** Seamlessly expands with `#F6F9F7` inset background or clean white separation to present detailed dental advice.

### Pricing & Membership Plan Cards
- **Layout:** Tiered cards with an accented top badge for recommended tiers. Features large monetary figures (`$49 / Month`), followed by a tick-list of included procedures styled with clover green checkmark icons.
