SEAMLESS PATIENT JOURNEY

What Happens After You Contact Us?

1

Immediate Clinical Triage

A dedicated receptionist reviews your clinical needs within 2 working hours. If symptoms suggest pulpitis, swelling, or infection, we prioritise same-day treatment.

2

Tailored Clinician Matching

We match your requirement with the right General Dental Council registered dentist or therapist (NHS Band 1-3 or private cosmetic clinician) at your preferred time.

3

Digital Paperwork & SMS Check-In

You receive an encrypted SMS/email to complete medical history from home on your phone — avoiding waiting room clipboard queues entirely.

favorite

Nervous or Dental Phobic?

Haven’t visited a dental surgery in years? Don’t worry — we never judge. Mention your dental anxiety in the message notes, and our team will book an extra unhurried consultation with our gentlest clinicians.

LOCATION & ACCESSIBILITY

Finding Us: Transport & Dedicated Parking

Situated on the bustling Warwick Road (A41), our dental practice is conveniently positioned for patients across Acocks Green, Hall Green, Solihull, and Greater Birmingham.

directions_car

By Car & Free Patient Parking

Located directly along Warwick Road (A41). We provide free, dedicated off-street patient parking spaces situated directly to the rear of the clinic building. Enter via the driveway adjacent to 1078 Warwick Road.

By Train & Public Bus

tram

Acocks Green Railway Station: Just a 5-minute walk (approx 350 yards) with direct regular rail links to Birmingham Moor Street, Birmingham Snow Hill, and Solihull Station.

directions_bus

Bus Services: National Express West Midlands routes 4, 4A, and the 11A / 11C Birmingham Outer Circle stop within 50 yards of our front entrance.

wheelchair_pickup

Step-Free Accessibility & Facilities

Our dental surgeries are located entirely on the ground floor. We provide a step-free ramp entrance, extra-wide clinic doors accommodating motorized wheelchairs and strollers, and fully accessible patient washrooms.
