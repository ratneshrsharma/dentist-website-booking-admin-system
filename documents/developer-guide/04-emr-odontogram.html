2. 5-Surface Tooth Condition Mapping

Each tooth is divided into 5 independent interactive geometric surfaces:

  • O (Occlusal / Incisal): Biting surface
  • M (Mesial): Surface facing midline
  • D (Distal): Surface facing back of mouth
  • B (Buccal / Facial): Surface facing cheek / lips
  • L (Lingual / Palatal): Surface facing tongue / roof of mouth
Condition Hex Color CDT Code Reference Clinical Meaning
caries #EF4444 (Red) Diagnostic Active dental decay requiring restorative excavation.
composite #3B82F6 (Blue) D2391 / D2392 Existing resin-based tooth-colored restoration.
crown #F59E0B (Amber) D2740 / D2750 Full-coverage ceramic / porcelain crown.
root_canal #8B5CF6 (Purple) D3310 / D3330 Endodontic obturation completed or indicated.
implant #0D9488 (Teal) D6010 Endosteal surgical titanium fixture placed.

3. Periodontal Probing Depth Tracking

Six measurement points per tooth (Distobuccal, Buccal, Mesiobuccal, Distolingual, Lingual, Mesiolingual). Values 1–3mm are considered healthy (green), 4mm requires monitoring (amber), and ≥5mm indicates active periodontitis requiring deep scaling and root planing (red alert flag).

5. Support Tickets & Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

TICKET-EMR-401 • Tooth condition resets to white after saving chart
AJAX / Payload

Symptom: Doctor marks Tooth #19 Occlusal surface as "caries", clicks Save, but on reload tooth is unshaded.

Root Cause: The AJAX payload sent surface: "O" in lowercase or missing CSRF token caused a silent 419 error.

Resolution Runbook:

  1. In odontogram.blade.php, ensure surface enums are uppercase (strtoupper(surface)).
  2. Verify headers include 'X-CSRF-TOKEN': $('meta[name="csrf-token"]').attr('content').
  3. Check MySQL tooth_records table for records where patient_id = :id.
