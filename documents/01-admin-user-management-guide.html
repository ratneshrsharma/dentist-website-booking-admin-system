1. System Security, Login & Authentication

The Advanced Dental PRO portal utilizes a zero-trust, role-based access control (RBAC) architecture designed to comply with UK NHS, CQC, HIPAA, and GDPR healthcare privacy directives.

Strict Data-Leak Prevention Directive: Non-clinical staff (Receptionists, Website Managers, Billing Officers) are strictly blocked from viewing confidential clinical dental charts, surgical periodontal depths, or medical diagnoses. Unauthorized route visits immediately trigger an HTTP 403 Forbidden response and are logged in the audit ledger.

Login Security Protocol

1

Manual Credential Entry

The login form loads with autocomplete="off" and completely blank fields. No passwords or clinical emails are cached in the browser form to prevent unauthorized terminal access in shared clinic operatories.

2

Session Lockdown

All administrative sessions are guarded with encrypted HTTP-only session cookies. If a staff member leaves a workstation idle for 30 minutes, the session is invalidated and redirected to the login gate.

3

Secure Sign-Out

Always click the user avatar in the top-right navbar and choose "Sign Out" when leaving the dental chair or front-desk reception counter.

2. Global Navbar Controls & Triage System

Once authenticated, the top navigation bar provides instant facility controls accessible across all clinical modules:

Multi-Branch Location Switcher

Located next to the sidebar toggle. Allows clinicians and managers to toggle between active practice facilities (e.g. Metro Dental HQ, West 57th Suite, North Surgery Center). EMR and calendar views reload instantly for that location.

Emergency Notification Bell

Features an aligned red alert badge indicating urgent action items: acute emergency walk-in triage, critical restorative material shortages (e.g. Straumann implants < 3), and settling POS receipts.

Staff Profile & Quick Role Switch

Displays current clinician name, role badge, and active work branch. Includes 1-click navigation to permitted workspaces and immediate session termination.

3. Super Administrator Operational Guide

Unrestricted Access

Primary Account: admin@example.com • Scope: Full Multi-Branch Clinical & Financial Governance.

Core Management Responsibilities:

  1. Multi-Clinic Facility Provisioning (/admin/clinics): Add new clinic branches, define operatory chair counts, assign facility lead clinicians, and configure individual branch opening hours and emergency WhatsApp numbers.
  2. Staff Provisioning & RBAC Permissions (/admin/roles): Create new clinical staff, assign one of the 6 granular roles, reset two-factor authentication (2FA), and audit active staff login sessions.
  3. Practice-Wide Financial & POS Oversight (/admin/billing): Review gross collections across all branches, audit pending insurance claims, inspect voided invoices, and export quarterly tax statements.
  4. Branding & Digital Infrastructure (/admin/theme-customizer & /admin/seo-manager): Approve global theme design tokens, upload custom clinic web fonts, inspect SEO health scores, and deploy 301 redirection rules for retired marketing campaigns.

4. Clinic Administrator / Practice Manager

Practice Operations

Primary Account: clinic.admin@example.com • Scope: Daily Branch Operations, Operatory Scheduling, Supplies.

Daily Workflow:

1

Morning Chair & Doctor Roster Audit

Open /admin/appointments/calendar. Verify that all operatories (Chairs 1–4) have an assigned attending clinician and dental nurse. Check for emergency triage overflow.

2

Supplies & Stock Audit (/admin/inventory)

Check the "Low Stock Warnings" card. Ensure restorative composites, anesthetic cartridges, and sterile surgical packs are above threshold. Generate supplier purchase orders directly from the inventory table.

3

Patient Intake Compliance

Open /admin/patients to ensure all new patients have completed digital medical history forms and allergy declarations prior to being seated in the dental operatory.

5. Doctor & Dental Surgeon (Clinical EMR)

Clinical Medical Records Only

Primary Account: dr.caldwell@example.com • Scope: Dental Charting, Odontogram EMR, Treatment Planning.

Privacy Isolation: When logged in as a Doctor, billing registers, SEO customizers, and staff role editors are automatically hidden from the navigation sidebar to maintain focus on clinical patient care.

Interactive Odontogram Charting Guide (/admin/emr/odontogram):

Tooth Selection

Interactive 32-tooth chart (Universal & ISO notation). Click any tooth to load its 5 individual surfaces: Occlusal, Mesial, Distal, Buccal, Lingual.

Clinical Condition Mapping

Select surface and assign condition:
• Caries / Decay (Red)
• Composite Filling (Blue)
• Crown / Onlay (Amber)
• Root Canal (Purple)
• Dental Implant (Teal)

Clinical Progress Notes

Type surgical notes, local anesthetic batch numbers (e.g. Septodont Lidocaine 2% with 1:100k epi), and attach CBCT 3D DICOM files. Click "Save EMR Record" to commit to patient history.

6. Receptionist / Appointment Intake Manager

Front Desk & Patient Intake

Primary Account: reception@example.com • Scope: Calendar, Patient Intake, WhatsApp Confirmations.

Operating the Scheduler & Calendar (/admin/appointments/calendar):

  • Booking New Appointments: Click the "+ New Appointment" button in top header. Select Patient, Attending Clinician, Treatment Type, Date, and Morning/Afternoon Time Slot.
  • Status Toggling: Click any appointment block on the calendar to mark its state: Confirmed → Checked-In (Waiting Room) → In Chair → Completed → Cancelled / No-Show.
  • Instant WhatsApp Notification: When an appointment is scheduled, click the WhatsApp action icon to trigger a direct confirmation message to the patient's phone (+919904379260) with clinic address and arrival instructions.

Patient Intake & Registration (/admin/patients):

Register new walk-in patients with full name, phone, email, date of birth, emergency contact, and medical alert flags (e.g. Penicillin Allergy, Latex Sensitivity, Type 2 Diabetes). A Medical Record Number (MRN) is assigned automatically.

7. Website CMS, Theme & SEO Manager

Digital Marketing & Branding

Primary Account: webmaster@example.com • Scope: CMS Pages, Theme Customizer, Yoast SEO, 301 Redirects.

1. Website CMS Pages Manager (/admin/cms-pages):

Manage all 24+ pre-rendered Next.js patient landing pages (Home, About Us, Clear Aligners, Dental Implants, Porcelain Veneers, Emergency Dentistry). Edit headlines, body content, and live publishing status without touching source code.

2. Theme & Design Tokens Customizer (/admin/theme-customizer):

  • Color Palette: Change Primary Brand Teal, Dark Obsidian Surface, and Warm Champagne accents dynamically. Changes sync immediately via the /api/v1/theme/settings endpoint.
  • Typography Hierarchy: Select primary and secondary web fonts, line heights, and font cases for h1 through h6.
  • WhatsApp Triage Number: Update the clinic's global WhatsApp number (+919904379260) in Section 1.5; all website buttons update automatically.

3. Yoast-Style SEO Suite & Redirects (/admin/seo-manager & /admin/seo-redirects):

Customize meta titles (50–60 chars), meta descriptions (150–160 chars), social OpenGraph cards, and inject JSON-LD MedicalBusiness structured data for high local Google Maps ranking. Add 301 Permanent Redirects for retired marketing URLs.

8. Billing, Invoices & POS Officer

Financial Operations & POS

Primary Account: billing@example.com • Scope: Invoicing, POS Settlements, WhatsApp Receipts, Tax Export.

Creating & Settling Invoices (/admin/billing):

1

Click "+ New POS Invoice"

Select Patient from the dropdown (or enter a new patient name), choose the attending clinician, select the procedure (e.g. Guided Implant Placement, Tooth Whitening), and enter the total settled amount.

2

Select Tender Method

Select tender: Visa / Mastercard POS, Cash, Direct Debit, Dental Insurance Copay, or Apple/Google Pay. Choose claim status: Paid in Full or Insurance Claim Pending.

3

Electronic WhatsApp Receipt Dispatch

Click the green WhatsApp button on any invoice row. The system opens a pre-composed message with a direct link to the public digital receipt:
https://demo.ratneshsharma.com/advanced-dental-clinic/billing/INV-2026-0891

4

Print Official Dental Tax Receipt

Click the printer icon or open the digital receipt. Click "Print Official Tax Receipt / Export PDF". The print stylesheet automatically hides navbar buttons and formats an official A4/Letter medical invoice with clinic VAT ID.
